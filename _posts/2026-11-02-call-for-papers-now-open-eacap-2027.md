We are pleased to announce that the Call for Papers for the inaugural East Asian Graduate Conference in Analytic Philosophy (EACAP 2027) is now open.

The conference will take place at Seoul National University (Seoul, South Korea) on June 16–18, 2027, and welcomes submissions from graduate students in all areas of analytic philosophy, broadly construed.

**Keynote Speakers**

- **Matthew Mandelkern** (New York University)
- **Kengo Miyazono** (Hokkaido University)

<p style="font-size: 13px; font-style: italic; font-weight: 400; color: #606a70; line-height: 1.5; margin: 12px 0 0;">* Further announcements to follow.</p>

The key dates are as follows:

- **Submission Deadline:** February 20, 2027
- **Authors Notified By:** April 10, 2027

For submission guidelines and further details, please see the [Call for Papers](/cfp/).
