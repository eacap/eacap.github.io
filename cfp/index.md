Thank you for your interest in EACAP 2027. Please read the **Submission Guidelines** carefully before submitting your paper. Submissions that do not comply with the submission guidelines may be disqualified from consideration.

EACAP welcomes submissions from current graduate students in all areas of analytic philosophy, broadly construed.

Submission Deadline: February 20, 2027

Authors Notified By: April 10, 2027

### Submission Formats

We invite submissions in two categories:

1. Paper Presentations

Each speaker will have 25–30 minutes to present their paper, followed by a Q&A session within a 50-minute slot.

Submissions must consist of a paper of no more than **3,000 words** (exclusive of footnotes and bibliography). 

WIP(Work-in-Progress) Forum

EACAP 2027 will also feature WIP Forums to encourage the exchange of ideas and constructive feedback on research in progress. Each forum consists of four 10-minute presentations, followed by a 40-minute coffee break during which presenters and participants can freely discuss the presented work.

Please submit an extended abstract of no more than **500 words** (exclusive of footnotes and bibliography), accompanied by an approx. 150-word abstract.

Submissions must consist of an extended abstract of no more than **500 words**.

### Submission Guidelines

- All submissions must be in PDF format and prepared for anonymous review. Please ensure that neither the file name nor the document itself contains any identifying information, including the author's name or institutional affiliation.
- Work published by the submission deadline will not be considered.
- All submissions must be accompanied by an approx. 150-word abstract.
- Only one submission per author is permitted. However, authors submitting to the Paper Presentation may indicate whether they would like their papers to be considered for the WIP Forum if not selected for a Paper Presentation. This option will appear when selecting 'Paper Presentation' on the submission form.
